About Belton, Texas

Belton is the seat of Bell County, located along I-35 between Waco and Georgetown, approximately 50 miles from Georgetown. The city features Belton Lake -- a 12,300-acre US Army Corps of Engineers reservoir -- Yettie Polk Park, the Nolan Creek Hike and Bike Trail, and the University of Mary Hardin-Baylor campus. Belton ISD serves the community with a strong academic tradition.

Key landmarks: Belton Lake, Yettie Polk Park, University of Mary Hardin-Baylor, Nolan Creek Hike and Bike Trail, Bell County Expo Center, I-35 Corridor, downtown Belton, Lake Belton
Schools: Belton High School, Lake Belton High School, Belton Middle School, South Belton Middle School, Leon Junction Elementary (Belton ISD)
