How Much Does a Bounce House Rental Cost in Georgetown TX in 2026?

📅 March 3, 2026 ⏰ 4 min read 📍 Georgetown TX

What Does a Bounce House Rental Cost in Georgetown TX?

Bounce house rentals in Georgetown TX start at $155 for a standard themed moonwalk and go up depending on size, features, and rental type. Here is the full breakdown of what you can expect to pay in 2026 for each category delivered to Georgetown TX and Williamson County.

Standard themed bounce houses -- the enclosed 15x15 ft inflatable jump castles in themes like Bluey, Frozen, Fortnite, and Cocomelon -- start at $155 and range up to $195 for premium themed units. Combo bounce houses, which combine a bounce area with a slide, basketball hoop, or climbing wall, start at $195 and range up to $295 for 6N1 combo units. Water slide rentals start at $245 for junior slides and go up to $1,095 for the 32ft Pirate Dual Lane mega slide. Obstacle course rentals start at $365 for a single-piece 34ft course and reach $1,595 for the 160ft Radical Run 5-piece mega run.

What Is Included in the Price?

Every bounce house rental price in Georgetown TX includes delivery to your address, full setup by our crew, all safety equipment, and pickup after your event. There are no delivery fees, setup fees, fuel surcharges, or hidden add-ons. What you are quoted is what you pay.

Our crew arrives before your event start time, sets up the inflatable completely, walks you through the safety guidelines, and returns after your event to break everything down. You need to provide a flat outdoor area, access to a standard 110v outlet within 50 feet, and a clear path to the setup location. Water slides also require a standard garden hose. We bring everything else.

What Affects the Price of a Bounce House Rental?

Three factors affect bounce house rental pricing in Georgetown TX: the type of unit, the size of the unit, and the distance of the delivery location.

Type matters most. A standard bounce house costs less than a combo, which costs less than a water slide, which costs less than a mega obstacle course. Size is the second factor -- a 15ft junior water slide is priced differently than a 27ft double lane mega slide. Distance plays a minor role for deliveries beyond the core Georgetown TX service area -- cities like Killeen TX and Belton TX are further from our base and may carry a small delivery adjustment. For all Georgetown TX ZIP codes -- 78626, 78628, and 78633 -- and nearby Williamson County cities, there is no additional delivery fee.
