The 7 Most Popular Bounce House Themes for Birthday Parties

📅 March 10, 2026 ⏰ 5 min read 📍 Georgetown TX

1. Bluey. The Reigning Champion

Bluey has been the single most-requested bounce house theme for Georgetown TX birthday parties for two consecutive years running. The Australian Blue Heeler puppy and her family resonate with kids ages 2 through 7 in a way few characters have matched. Both the Bluey Boy Banner and Bluey Girl bounce houses are consistently booked out weeks in advance in Wolf Ranch and Teravista during party season.

If your child is in the 3-to-6 age range and watches any streaming content at all, there is a strong chance Bluey tops the list. Book early, this one fills up fastest.

2. Frozen. The Evergreen Classic

Frozen has remarkable staying power as a bounce house theme. Years after the films released, Elsa and Anna remain consistently requested for girls' birthday parties across Georgetown TX and Williamson County. The Frozen Girl Birthday Bounce House is one of our most-booked units in Berry Creek, Rancho Sienna, and Cimarron Hills.

Frozen works especially well for ages 4 through 9. If your daughter has moved past the peak Frozen phase, consider the Princess Castle bounce house as an alternative, same regal theme without the specific IP.

3. Fortnite. The Older Kids Favorite

Fortnite is consistently the top-requested theme for the 8-through-14 crowd across Georgetown TX. The Fortnite Bouncy House Moonwalk brings the game's recognizable artwork into the backyard and is a reliable crowd-pleaser for birthday parties where older kids and tweens are the main guests.

It is worth noting that Fortnite also works well for mixed-gender groups, unlike many princess or character themes, Fortnite appeals broadly to both boys and girls in the upper elementary and middle school age range. The Fortnite Combo Bounce House with slide is the premium version and is popular for larger Georgetown TX parties.

4. Baby Shark. The Toddler Party Staple

Baby Shark is the go-to theme for birthday parties where the guest of honor is 2 to 4 years old. The recognizable characters and color palette make it instantly exciting for toddlers, and parents love the gentle, safe scale of the standard Baby Shark bounce house which is sized perfectly for the youngest jumpers.

In Georgetown TX communities like Wolf Ranch and Teravista where there is a high concentration of young families, Baby Shark and Paw Patrol are the two most-requested themes for the under-5 birthday crowd.

5. Cocomelon. For the Youngest Party Guests

Cocomelon is the dominant theme for the 1-to-3 age group. If you are hosting a first or second birthday party in Georgetown TX, Cocomelon bounce houses and combo units are consistently the top pick. The Cocomelon Bounce House Combo, which can operate wet or dry, is the most popular Cocomelon unit and a favorite for warm-weather Georgetown TX parties in Rancho Sienna and Morningstar.

One practical note: at this age the bounce house is as much for slightly older siblings and cousins as it is for the birthday child themselves. The Cocomelon unit handles both audiences well.

6. Avengers. The Superhero Party Leader

The Avengers Modular Bounce House and Avengers Mod Moonwalk Combo are consistently the top-requested superhero themes for Georgetown TX birthday parties. Spider-Man and Batman are close behind. For boys aged 5 through 12, superhero themes remain one of the most reliable party hits year after year.

The Avengers Combo is particularly popular for Georgetown ISD school-age birthday parties in Berry Creek and Cimarron Hills where the party guest list skews older and more active. The combo's climbing wall and basketball hoop add engagement for kids who are past the pure bouncing phase.

7. Gender-Neutral Options. The Safe Default

When you are not sure about the theme, or when your party has a wide age range and mixed genders, the gender-neutral options are your best bet. The Caution Bounce House, a bold yellow and black design with a fun warning tape aesthetic, and the Happy Birthday bounce house are the two most-booked non-character units in Georgetown TX.

Both work for ages 3 through 14, fit any party color scheme, and photograph well. They are also typically available on shorter notice than the in-demand character themes since they do not have the same peak demand. If you are booking last-minute for a Georgetown TX party, these are the most reliable options.
