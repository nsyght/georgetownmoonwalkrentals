What is a moonwalk and how is it different from a bounce house?

Moonwalk and bounce house are two names for the same rental. In Texas, inflatable jump houses are traditionally called moonwalks. Other common names include jumper, jump house, bouncy castle, and spacewalk. Georgetown Moonwalk Rentals delivers all of these -- same structure, same prices starting at $155.
