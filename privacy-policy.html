Privacy Policy

How Georgetown Moonwalk Rentals collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Overview

Georgetown Moonwalk Rentals ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website georgetownmoonwalkrentals.com. All inflatable rental services are provided by Jump Around Party Rentals, a licensed and insured operator based in Round Rock, Texas. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when visiting our website or submitting a rental inquiry.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you submit a booking request, contact form, or rental inquiry through our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address or neighborhood
  • Event date and rental preferences
  • Any additional details you include in your message

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and coordinate your rental booking with Jump Around Party Rentals.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit georgetownmoonwalkrentals.com, certain information may be collected automatically by our hosting provider (Netlify) and standard web technologies, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and time of visit. This information is used for website performance monitoring and is not sold or shared with third-party advertisers.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your rental inquiry or booking request
  • Coordinate delivery and service details with Jump Around Party Rentals
  • Communicate availability, pricing, and scheduling information
  • Improve our website content and user experience

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your information is shared only with Jump Around Party Rentals for the purpose of fulfilling your rental request.

Contact Forms and Netlify Forms

Our website uses Netlify Forms to process contact and booking form submissions. Form submissions are stored securely by Netlify and forwarded to our team. Netlify's privacy policy is available at netlify.com/privacy. We retain form submissions for a reasonable period to facilitate follow-up communication and then delete them.

Cookies

Georgetown Moonwalk Rentals does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies. Our website may use essential session cookies required for basic website functionality. We do not run advertising networks or retargeting programs on this website.

Google Maps

Our website embeds Google Maps iframes on certain pages to display neighborhood and city delivery areas. When you view these maps, Google may collect information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not control Google's data collection practices.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our website, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide through our website. Our website is hosted on Netlify's secure infrastructure with HTTPS encryption. However, no method of internet transmission is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of any future communications from us

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us
